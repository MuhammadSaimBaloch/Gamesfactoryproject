## Research and assets

Product facts and source URLs are in [catalogue-content.json](../db/catalogue-content.json). Photo URLs, credits and processing notes are in [inventory-image-sources.json](../db/inventory-image-sources.json). Manufacturer images illustrate the model/design rather than the cosmetic condition or accessories of an individual pre-owned unit. Unconfirmed warranty and store-package claims were not invented.

Final source assets are under `public/catalogue/inventory-*.webp`. For the 30 isolated cutouts, the built-in image tool was used with this prompt pattern: “Remove only the background, floor, cast shadows and reflections. Preserve the original exact-model product, perspective, colours, materials, artwork, logos, text and buttons. No redesign or invented hardware. Genuine transparent alpha with small padding.” Where needed, the prompt identified only the product to extract from its packaging or surrounding promotional artwork. Native renders were trimmed, resized and compressed without background generation.

Apply migrations before serving the new catalogue. Existing deployment scripts apply forward migrations; local migration uses `pnpm db:migrate`. On a newly provisioned database, load the base catalogue SQL before the completion migration so all catalogue rows exist to receive the reviewed content. Never replay catalogue import scripts as an inventory synchronisation process.
